Tea Mingling Sessions with Faculty

Chitchat over coffee with our Faculty members!

Still not decided on which major you are interested in? Come and join us at the Tea Mingling Session to find out more. There are faculty members from different department who are happy to share more details of their specialised majors to you. Take some snacks, sit around, have a chat with a faculty member, ask all the questions you have to get an answer to.

Guided Lab Tours

Wanna know more about what the major programs do? Join the lab tours!

The Guided Laboratory Tour is another highlight in the Experience the major's series. Through this event, students can gain an in-depth understanding of the technologies, tools, and techniques used in different majors. These tours provide students with the opportunity to explore the labs, engage with faculty members and researchers, and learn more about the research related to the different majors.

Peer Consultation

Ask fellow peer mentors about their experience!

Peer Consultation is a part of the Engineering InfoWeek event where students can connect with peer mentors (or other senior students) and have conversations. Students can ask questions about different engineering majors, learn about the student experience, and receive guidance and support from their peers.

Current students will set booths for different Major Programs at the Engineering Commons. Feel free to ask any questions you may have about your ideal major!
