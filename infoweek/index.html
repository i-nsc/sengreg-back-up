Event Details

Register for activities

Are you a Year 1 engineering student eager to make an informed decision about choosing your major? Look no further than Engineering InfoWeek 2024! This week-long event is designed to help you explore your options and discover your passion.

The week starts with a Major Selection Briefing that details the process of choosing your major. Important information abounds! There will be Guided Lab Tours* where you can see first-hand what students do in each major.

If you want some face-to-face quality time for some chitchat over drinks, the Tea Mingling Sessions welcome everyone to chat with faculty members from each department to get a better understanding of each major.

Don't forget about the fun activities! By participating in the events, you can also collect stamps to play the Claw Machine# and win prizes. Want a lucky charm? Limited amounts only!

Engineering InfoWeek 2024 is the perfect opportunity for you to explore your interests, gain valuable insights into different majors, and have some fun along the way. Mark your calendars now and make the most of this incredible week – we can't wait to see you there!

*The laboratory tour sessions have limited seats and requires registration.

#Available on 22 and 23 Feb outside of LT-K and LT-J.
