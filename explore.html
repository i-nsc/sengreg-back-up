  • HMAW1905B – Behaviour Foundations of University Education: Habits, Mindset and Wellness [ 1 credit in Fall 2024 ]
  • English Communication (E-Comm)[ 3 credits ]
  • MATH1012 / MATH1013 – Calculus IA / Calculus IB [ 4 or 3 credits ]

Please note the following additional details:

  • Credit transfers may be applicable.

  • All new students will be pre-enrolled in an E-Comm course (LANG1402 / LANG1407) in Fall 2024-25 based on your submitted public examination result.
