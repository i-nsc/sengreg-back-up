NEW STUDENT ORIENTATION 2024

Attention, Future Engineers: Your Journey Begins Here!

Welcome to the School of Engineering (SENG), where we are dedicated to ensuring your smooth transition into university life. To make this experience truly unforgettable, we have curated a series of captivating events exclusively tailored for you. It is important that you attend ALL the events according to your type of admission (JUPAS/local, non-JUPAS/non-local).

Event details for senior entry students will be informed by their program offices.

Here are a series of orientation activities planned for you to help you get to know more about the School of Engineering and your curriculum. Mark down your calendar and make sure you don’t miss them!

Peer Mentoring Program (COMPULSORY)

Mid Aug to late Aug

Each new student will be assigned to one peer mentor who will provide support and advice to first year students. Peer Mentors will organise house orientation activities to welcome first year students and assist students in their transition into university life.

For more information about the SENG Peer Mentoring Program https://e2i.hkust.edu.hk/peer-mentoring-program
