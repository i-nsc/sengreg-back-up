School Welcome and Orientation


School Welcome and Orientation is SENG’s official undergraduate activity.

This event is hosted by our passionate staff of Center for Engineering Education Innovation (E²I) and a team of energetic peer mentors. Don't miss out this unique opportunity for networking, team building, and getting familiar with HKUST engineering community!


Details are as follows:

Date:28 August 2024 (Wed)
10:00 AM - 5:30 PM
Venue:HKUST S. H. Ho Sports Hall
